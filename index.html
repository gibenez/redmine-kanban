Sprint board · Quick guide

Choose your Sprint
Select a Project and Sprint. Commitment totals always include the whole Sprint, even when you filter.
Plan and update work
Drag a Card into a Sprint Column to plan it and set its status. Move it to Backlog to remove it from the selected Sprint, without resetting its status. Use the pencil button for keyboard-accessible planning, assignment and estimates.
Find an issue
Search by subject or exact issue ID, such as #123. Combine Tracker and Assignee filters to narrow the results. Scroll each Column independently; Backlog loads more as you scroll.
Widen the Backlog
The eye button switches between Unplanned issues in the New status and all open Unplanned issues.
Planning with Build Sprints
Choose a Category for its Build Sprints. The C button limits Backlog to that Category. Removing an issue from the selected Build Sprint preserves its other Build Sprint Memberships.
Permissions stay yours
Closed Sprints are read only. Redmine checks your permissions and workflow; changes appear only after they are confirmed. Open an issue ID to view its details in Redmine.
